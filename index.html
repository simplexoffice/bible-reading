Chronological Bible Reading

Era 3 — Judges to United Kingdom

Sessions 90–177 0 / 88

The judges, Ruth, Samuel, Saul, David, and Solomon. Psalms with event superscriptions are read at the moment in David's life they describe; the remaining psalms are read in blocks after David organizes the temple music (1 Ch 25). Era 5 — Exile & Return

Sessions 240–275 0 / 36

Ezekiel prophesied among the exiles from 613 B.C.E., overlapping Jerusalem's final years; Daniel served in Babylon's court from the first deportation to the Persian era. Then the return (537), the rebuilt temple (515), Esther in Persia, Ezra (468), Nehemiah (455), and Malachi's closing words. The genealogies of 1 Chronicles 1–9 are read here, where Ezra compiled them for the restored nation.
